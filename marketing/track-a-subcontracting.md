# Track A — Subcontracting outreach (SIs / MSPs / staffing / boutique partners)

**Goal:** get added to a partner's bench / a 15-min intro. This is partner-to-partner — warmer and more direct than cold end-client email. Fastest path to revenue.

**Audience / personas:** delivery managers, practice leads, resource/staffing managers, and owners at:
- Microsoft staffing & recruiting firms (Nigel Frank, Kforce, Apex)
- Boutique Dynamics/Power Platform partners (DCG, TopNotch, regional SIs)
- MSPs ($1–10M rev) whose clients ask for M365 automation they can't staff

**Cadence:** 3 touches — Day 0, Day 3, Day 8.

- Also register directly on contractor databases (Nigel Frank, Kforce) and in VMS portals (Fieldglass, Beeline) — that's inbound that complements this outbound.
